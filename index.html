FAQ

Questions, answered

Which image sizes does ShowcaseKit export?

iPhone exports at 1284 × 2778 px and iPad at 2232 × 2976 px. iPhone Duo exports at 2007 × 2853 / 2853 × 2007 px (inner) and 1398 × 2034 / 2034 × 1398 px (cover). Mac projects export at 2880 × 1800, 2560 × 1600, 1440 × 900 or 1280 × 800 px. App Store creative headers (21:9 · 3840 × 1646, 16:9 · 5244 × 2950) and search results artwork (3:2 · 3840 × 2560 or 1920 × 1280, 16:9 · 5244 × 2950) export as opaque PNGs.

Does ShowcaseKit collect or upload my screenshots?

No. ShowcaseKit has no accounts, no analytics and no network access. All projects, screenshots and logos are stored locally in ShowcaseKit’s sandboxed container on your Mac.

Where are my projects saved and how do I back them up?

Projects save automatically on your Mac. To back up a project or move it to another Mac, choose File › Export Project… to save a portable .showcasekit.json file, then open it with File › Import Project….

Is it compatible with PreviewKit?

Yes. .showcasekit.json uses the same format as PreviewKit’s .previewkit.json, so iOS and Android phone/tablet projects move freely between the two.

App Store Connect rejects my image—what should I check?

Verify that your project’s device class (iPhone, iPad, iPhone Duo or Mac) matches the slot you are uploading to in App Store Connect. If it still fails, check the Support page or open a GitHub issue with the exact error message.

What are the system requirements?

macOS 15.0 or later. ShowcaseKit is a native SwiftUI Mac app sold through the Mac App Store.
