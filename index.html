App Store screenshots, designed on your Mac

ShowcaseKit turns your app’s screenshots into polished App Store marketing images with device mockups, headlines and gradients, then exports them at the exact sizes App Store Connect expects.

What you can make

  • Screenshot sets for iPhone, iPad and foldable iPhone displays, in portrait or landscape.
  • Mac screenshots in a MacBook Pro or MacBook Air mockup, at 2880 × 1800, 2560 × 1600, 1440 × 900 or 1280 × 800 px.
  • Product page headers and search results artwork with your logo, a short phrase and device mockups.
  • Four layouts, eleven device models, gradient and solid backgrounds, and eleven typefaces.
  • One-click export of a single image, a ZIP of a whole set, or a folder.

Everything stays on your Mac. ShowcaseKit has no accounts, no analytics and no network access.
