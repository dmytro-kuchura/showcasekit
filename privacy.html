Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

ShowcaseKit does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. ShowcaseKit has no user accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking of any kind, and it does not connect to the internet.

Your files

The projects you create, and the screenshots, logos and artwork you add to them, are stored only on your Mac, inside ShowcaseKit’s sandboxed app container. ShowcaseKit reads and writes other files only when you choose them in an open or save dialog, or drag them into the app. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Purchases

ShowcaseKit is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple processes the purchase under Apple’s Privacy Policy; the developer does not receive your payment details.

Support requests

If you contact support through GitHub Issues, the information you choose to post is public and handled under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Don’t include personal information you don’t want to share publicly.

Children

ShowcaseKit does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
